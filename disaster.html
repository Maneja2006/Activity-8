Disaster Safety Reminder

Follow official evacuation instructions. Stay away from floodwaters, damaged buildings, downed power lines, and areas at risk of landslides. During an earthquake, drop, cover, and hold on. After shaking stops, move carefully to a safe open area if needed.

If a local hotline does not connect, try another listed contact or call 911. Hotline numbers may change, so verify them with the city government before relying on them in an emergency.
