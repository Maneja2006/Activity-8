Fire Safety Reminder

For an active fire or immediate danger, call 911. Evacuate immediately, stay away from smoke, and do not re-enter a burning building. If a local station's direct number does not connect, call 911.

Station listings and direct contact numbers can change. Confirm local numbers with the Bureau of Fire Protection or the relevant city government before relying on them.
