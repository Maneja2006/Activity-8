Medical Emergency Reminder

For a serious or life-threatening emergency, call 911 immediately.

When calling for an ambulance or medical assistance, provide your exact location, the patient's condition, the number of people who need help, and your callback number.

If one hospital or local hotline does not answer, try another listed emergency contact or call 911.

Hospital telephone numbers and services may change. Verify the contact details with the hospital or city government when possible.
